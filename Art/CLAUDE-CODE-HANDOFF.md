## Walk loader contract

- Every new walk sheet is **552×295**, three **184×295** cells.
- Frame order: **stride / neutral / opposite stride**. Idle index **1**; cycle **[0,1,2,1]**. The old comment saying neutral comes first contradicts both art and executable constants.
- Each normalized figure has top row **16**, last content row **277** (bottom exclusive 278), height **262**. Approved source masters were not resized in place.
- Right sheets are generated by mirroring **each left cell**, preserving cell order. Do not reverse the whole strip or mirror again at rendering time.
- Front/back strides step in depth; profiles show forward/rear movement.
- The studied `drawChar` defaults to **U1(38)** frame width, not the brief's 45. Scale by **184**, not sheet width 552. Verify current callers before integration.

## Colour-key contract

Background is exact **#FF00FF**. The loader removes pixels when **both** `r-g > 60` and `b-g > 60`. Surviving-figure margin is `60 - max(min(r-g, b-g))`; all delivered animation frames/poses have at least **8** headroom. Purple/rose shopper masters 25 and 32 also reopen at margin 8, zero holes and one component.

Do not revert to an absolute purple threshold or reverse the margin formula. Recheck after any colour-changing export, quantization or resampling. Some shipped office references themselves contain enclosed key defects; they are style references, not key-quality templates.
